# Research Limitations and Methodological Boundaries

This document discloses known empirical, geographical, econometric, and operational limitations of the AgriStress Analytics system. Transparent disclosure of these constraints is essential for maintaining scientific integrity during academic review.

---

## 1. Observational Association vs. Economic Causation

> **Core Principle:** Correlation does not imply economic causation.

A statistically significant association (low p-value or high correlation coefficient) between commodity price volatility and Kisan Call Centre queries does not prove that falling commodity prices directly *caused* farmers to call.
- **Confounding Variables:** Common external factors—such as severe weather events (monsoon arrival, drought, unseasonal rainfall, hailstorms)—simultaneously impact both agricultural mandi prices (through supply shocks) and farmer inquiry volumes (through crop damage concerns).
- **Observational Nature:** Because the data is observational rather than derived from a controlled randomized experiment, regression coefficients and correlation statistics must be interpreted purely as predictive associations, not structural macroeconomic multipliers.

---

## 2. Geographic and Spatial Aggregation Mismatches

- **District vs. State Grain:**
  - KCC call logs and APMC Mandi price records are often collected at the district or market yard level.
  - In contrast, retail diesel selling prices published by PPAC are maintained at state capital or metropolitan hub levels.
  - Broadcast joining state-level fuel prices to district-level queries introduces aggregation bias, assuming that every agricultural block within a state faces identical retail fuel pricing.
- **Interstate Market Influx:**
  - Farmers near state borders frequently sell produce in neighboring state mandis or place calls to alternative regional call centers. Spatial boundaries do not strictly isolate agricultural marketing behavior.

---

## 3. Data Availability and Retail Price Limitations

- **Wholesale Dominance:**
  - The Agmarknet portal primarily tracks wholesale mandi modal prices across several hundred agricultural commodities.
  - Retail price monitoring is conducted separately by the Department of Consumer Affairs (Price Monitoring Cell) for only 22 essential commodities across selected urban centers.
  - Consequently, **wholesale-to-retail price spreads cannot be universally computed for all crops or regions**. When retail price observations are absent, the application explicitly refuses to fabricate synthetic spreads.

---

## 4. Query Demand vs. Actual Staffing Mandates

- **Answered vs. Attempted Calls:**
  - KCC logs in open government repositories reflect successfully connected and logged calls.
  - The metrics do not directly record caller queue abandonment, busy tone rejections, or network dropped calls during peak congestion hours.
- **Advisory-Stress Indicator Purpose:**
  - The `Normal`, `Elevated`, and `High` stress classifications are **project-defined indicators** calibrated against historical demand distributions.
  - They provide situational awareness for operational planning, but **must not be misconstrued as official government staffing directives** or civil service workforce mandates.

---

## 5. Forecasting Uncertainty and Non-Stationary Structural Breaks

- **Short-Term Predictive Validity:**
  - SARIMAX models assume that past patterns of trend, autocorrelation, and 7-day seasonality persist into the near future.
  - Unexpected policy interventions (such as export bans, minimum support price revisions, import tariff modifications) or sudden extreme climate anomalies represent structural breaks that statistical time series models cannot anticipate purely from past lag values.
- **Prediction Intervals:**
  - Forecast bounds widen significantly as the horizon extends beyond 14 days. Decisions should prioritize short-to-medium horizons (7 to 21 days).

---

## 6. Synthetic Demonstration Data Caution

- When running the application using demo files generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py`, all outputs are strictly pedagogical.
- The resulting correlation coefficients and p-values illustrate the mathematical execution of the software pipeline, but **do not constitute real-world empirical findings**.
